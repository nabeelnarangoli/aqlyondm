## What to Expect from a Social Media Management Company in Dubai

Posting now and then isn't the same as managing your social media. Here's how a typical month works with us:

1.  **Plan.** We agree on goals, themes and key dates, such as offers, launches, Ramadan, Diwali, Onam and national days.
2.  **Create.** Our designers and video team produce posts, reels and stories in your brand style, in English and other languages when you need them.
3.  **Approve.** You see everything before it goes live, in one simple calendar.
4.  **Publish and reply.** We post at the times your audience is most active and answer comments and messages quickly.
5.  **Promote.** Small, well-targeted ads put your best posts in front of new people in the UAE.
6.  **Report.** Each month you get a short report on reach, engagement, followers and enquiries, with next steps.

Meta's [Business Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/business/help) explains the tools behind Facebook and Instagram if you want to learn more. You don't need to master them yourself, though. That's our job.
