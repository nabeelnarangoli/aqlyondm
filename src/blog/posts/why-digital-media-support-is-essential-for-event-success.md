## What to Look for in a Poster Design Company in Dubai

The event poster is often the first thing people see, usually on WhatsApp or Instagram. A good one does a lot of work. Here's what makes it succeed:

-   **One clear message.** The event name, date, time and venue should be readable in two seconds on a phone.
-   **A clear hierarchy.** Guests, sponsors and logos matter. They shouldn't fight with the main message.
-   **Every size, ready.** You'll need versions for Instagram posts, stories, WhatsApp, LED screens and print.
-   **Languages done right.** Many Dubai events need English plus Malayalam, Arabic or Hindi. Each script needs proper typesetting.
-   **A matching family.** Countdown posts, speaker cards and stage backdrops should all look like one event.

Timing matters too. Share the main poster three to four weeks before the event, then follow with speaker cards, countdowns and a final reminder the day before. On the day, switch to live photos and short videos. That rhythm keeps people talking about your event before, during and after it.
